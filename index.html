Questions? We got BOO.

Can I request my own fuzzy wire character?

Yes! Choose a product marked “Customizable,” pick a character and colors, and share your idea in the notes. The team will confirm what can be made before accepting your order.

What is inside a mystery pouch?

That’s the surprise! Contents vary. We check that every pouch is complete, clean, packed securely, and in good condition before selling.

Does checking out here confirm my order?

Not automatically. The site prepares an order request. If online submissions have been enabled by the team, you’ll see a successful submission message; otherwise, save your order slip and coordinate with the booth team. Stock, total price, and payment are confirmed in person.

Can I have my order delivered?

Our current website focuses on school-booth pickup at Taguig National High School. Ask our team directly if any other arrangements are available.

Where can I join JengaBOO or Minute to Win It?

Visit the Charmistry Co. booth inside Taguig National High School, beside the “I Love TNHS” signage. Game rules and participation details are explained at the booth.
